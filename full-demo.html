How this video was made#

  • It joins the ten step videos of this guide, unchanged, with an opening and a summary. Steps 2 to 4 were recorded in the Azure portal and GitHub; steps 1 and 5 to 9 show the real automated run (public GitHub pages, pipeline output and the live app); clean-up was not run.
  • The narration uses Azure neural voices.
  • Subtitles follow the narration script, and their timing is approximate.
  • The publish workflow joins the clips with scripts/guide/build_full_video.py (video stream copied, one continuous audio track, MP4 chapters), so the videos always match the step videos.
